What is ImpactHack?
Forget candlelit dinners: this Valentine's weekend, show some love to problems that matter.
ImpactHack is a 24-hour, student-led global hackathon taking place on 13–14 February
2027 across the University of Bristol’s Temple Quarter Enterprise and Mumbai Enterprise
Campuses. We’re bringing together over 300 student hackers to tackle real-world
challenges using data science and AI. Expect big ideas, copious energy drinks, and a
bouquet of roses for the winning team. Who said romance was dead?

What are the benefits of being a sponsor?
    Promote your brand across two continents in one weekend.
    Meet talented students in two cities for internships and graduate roles: Bristol, one
    of the UK’s leading technology hubs, and Mumbai, India’s financial capital.
    Be part of one of Bristol’s biggest ever hackathons, and one of the first major
    events at the new Temple Quarter Enterprise Campus, a state-of-the-art space for
    students, startups, researchers and industry.
    Put your tools and APIs in the hands of hundreds of builders for 24 hours.

How you can sponsor ImpactHack
    Direct financial sponsorship. Allows us to spend your contribution where it is
    most needed, pooling it with other contributions to cover larger costs such as
    prizes, food and travel.
    In-kind sponsorship. Cover meals, snacks or drinks (e.g. “Breakfast brought to
    you by [Company]”), transport to the venue, or extra tools and credits for
    participants. You can also sponsor a non-monetary prize, such as software
    credits, a guaranteed interview or an office visit.

Our track record
ImpactHack might be our most ambitious hackathon yet, but it’s far from our first. We’ve
run several day hacks at the University of Bristol with around 100 participants each. We’ve
also competed in plenty of hackathons ourselves, winning a few, and taken part in multi-
city hacks like HackEurope. We know what works and what doesn’t, but we also know we
don’t have all the answers. That’s why we’ve partnered with some of the UK’s leading
hackathon organisers to make this a standout event for students across London, the
